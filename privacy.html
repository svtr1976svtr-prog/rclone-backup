Privacy Policy

Rclone Backup is a privately operated backup integration for transferring authorized backup files to Google Drive.

The application accesses Google Drive only after the account owner grants permission through Google OAuth.

Backup files and authentication credentials are not published on this website.

Google Drive data is used only for backup storage, synchronization and restoration.

Google account information is not sold or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
